Section 29 of the Electricity Regulation Act

The Ministerial determination power for new generation capacity — and how it should work once there is a wholesale market.

What section 29 does

Section 29 is how the Minister, in consultation with NERSA, determines that new generation capacity is needed, of what type, and through which buyer (historically Eskom, then the IPP Office). Every REIPPPP round and the gas IPP programme sits on a s29 determination.

Why NERSA is consulting now

In a SAWEM world generators can also enter via the market, not only via a Ministerial determination. The consultation is about the boundary: when does the state still procure for security of supply, and when does the market build? Get this wrong and you either crowd out private plant or you leave a capacity hole the market will not fill.

What to watch

Comments closed 3 August. No decision-meeting date is published. Read against IRP 2025 (the capacity plan s29 is meant to implement) and the Market Code (the other entry route).
