💰 National Treasury

Public finance & fiscal policy

📑 Overview

The National Treasury is the national department responsible for managing South Africa's public finances. It develops and implements fiscal policy, manages the national budget, and oversees the financial management of all organs of state.

📄 Functions
  • Preparing and managing the national budget
  • Developing and implementing fiscal and tax policy
  • Managing government debt and borrowing
  • Overseeing the financial management of public entities and municipalities
  • Regulating public procurement
  • Coordinating the fiscal framework and intergovernmental fiscal relations
