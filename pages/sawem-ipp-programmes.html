PPA Price (R/MWh) is the as-bid fully indexed 20-year Eskom strike (or BESIPPPP capital-cost recovery charge) at that bid window’s base date. Named preferred-bidder tariffs are used where the IPP Office published a project table; otherwise the official window-and-technology average is applied and labelled in Notes. 📋 What makes up the IPP capacity?

The complete IPP picture in one registry

The plants.csv registry separates three levels so totals never double-count:

  • Project — an actual physical plant (e.g. Cookhouse Wind Farm 138.6 MW, Kaxu Solar One 100 MW).
  • Allocation — an awarded programme amount not yet broken into named projects (e.g. BESIPPPP BW2 616 MW, the RMIPPPP balance).
  • Target — a published procurement target before award (REIPPPP BW7 remaining 1 060 MW of the 5 000 MW envelope, GIPPPP 2 000 MW, BESIPPPP BW3).

REIPPPP allocated about 6,000+ MW across BW1–BW6 (the registry lists the named projects that appear in available project sources — dominated by wind, solar PV and CSP). BW7 appointed 18 solar PV preferred bidders totalling 3 940 MW (20-year Eskom PPA; published strike in R/MWh); 1 060 MW of the 5 000 MW envelope remains unawarded and no wind preferred bidders were named. RMIPPPP (2020) awarded 1,995 MW across 12 projects for dispatchable gas/diesel, battery and hybrid capacity. BESIPPPP (2024–) awarded 360 MW (BW1, 1440 MWh) and 616 MW (BW2); BW3 announced. GIPPPP (2023) is targeting 2,000 MW of gas; the Bid Window 1 list of bids received (29 May 2026) comprises 4 bids totalling 2,830 MW (see the row-level bids below).

📋 Programme Details

REIPPPP — Renewable Energy IPP Procurement Programme

2011–present

Competitive auctions for onshore wind, solar PV, CSP, small hydro, biomass, landfill gas and biogas. Bid windows were halted between 2015–2019 (blamed for worsening load shedding). BW7 opened as a 5,000 MW rolling window (3,200 MW wind + 1,800 MW solar). Three preferred-bidder rounds appointed 18 solar PV projects (3,940 MW) on 20-year Eskom PPAs; no wind preferred bidders. 1,060 MW of the envelope remains unawarded. PPA prices are the evaluated bid tariffs in R/MWh.

RMIPPPP — Risk Mitigation IPP Procurement Programme

2020

Introduced to quickly bring ~2,000 MW of dispatchable capacity online. Technology-neutral: gas/diesel reciprocating engines, battery storage and hybrid projects. 12 preferred bidders, 1,995 MW total (incl. the Coega Karpowership, Mulilo Coega gas plant, Dassiesridge wind and a remaining balance row).

BESIPPPP — Battery Energy Storage IPP Procurement Programme

2024–present

Dedicated grid-scale battery storage. BW1 (Nov 2024): 2 projects, 1,440 MWh / 360 MW. BW2 (May 2025): 5 projects, 616 MW. BW3 announced 30 May 2025.

GIPPPP — Gas IPP Procurement Programme

2023

First dedicated gas-to-power programme. RFP issued 14 Dec 2023 for 2,000 MW; bids submitted 30 Aug 2024. The Bid Window 1 list of bids received was published on 29 May 2026 — 4 bids totalling 2,830 MW (2 × LNG, 2 × natural gas). No preferred bidders announced yet.
