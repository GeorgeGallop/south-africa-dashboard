OBTAINING IMAGES
================

Public Domain/Creative Commons Sources:
- Wikimedia Commons: commons.wikimedia.org (search for South Africa, Eskom, coal)
- Free Stock Photos: Unsplash, Pixabay, Pexels (for blackout/energy transition concepts)
- Government Sources: DMRE (Department of Mineral Resources), Statistics SA
- News Archives: Reuters, AP Photo, BBC have licensed coal/Eskom images

Usage: Ensure proper attribution in HTML comments or captions. Most Wikimedia images 
can be freely used with attribution to the photographer/source.
