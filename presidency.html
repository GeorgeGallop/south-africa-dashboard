🏳️‍🇵🇩 The Presidency

Executive leadership of the Republic of South Africa

📑 Overview

The Presidency is the executive arm of the South African government, headed by the President of the Republic. It coordinates and oversees the implementation of government policy across all national departments. The Presidency includes the Deputy President, the Minister in the Presidency, and supporting departments.

📄 Functions
  • Executive leadership and national agenda-setting
  • Coordination of government policy implementation
  • Appointment of cabinet ministers and directors-general
  • Management of the national budget and resource allocation
  • Representation of South Africa internationally (head of state functions)
  • Oversight of state security and intelligence services
  • Signing of bills passed by Parliament into law
📚 Structure

The Presidency comprises several key components:

  • The President — Head of State and Head of Government
  • The Deputy President — Assists the President and leads specific policy areas
  • Minister in the Presidency — Coordinates policy implementation and parliamentary programmes
  • Department of Planning, Monitoring and Evaluation (DPME) — Tracks government performance
  • State Security Agency (SSA) — National intelligence
