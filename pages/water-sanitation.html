💧 Department of Water & Sanitation

Water services & sanitation

📑 Overview

The Department of Water and Sanitation (DWS) is the national department responsible for the management of South Africa's water resources and the provision of water and sanitation services. It ensures the sustainable use of water and drives water infrastructure investment.

📄 Functions
  • Managing the country's water resources (rivers, dams, groundwater)
  • Regulating water use and issuing water licences
  • Promoting the provision of basic water and sanitation services
  • Protecting water quality and the aquatic environment
  • Investing in bulk water infrastructure and dam construction
  • Coordinating the response to droughts and water scarcity
