⚠️ Important: Design vs Actual Performance

The efficiency and heat rate figures above are design values at full load using design-grade coal. In reality, Eskom's coal fleet operates at significantly lower efficiency than design for several reasons:

  • Coal quality: Design grade coal has ~25–27 MJ/kg calorific value. Eskom now often receives coal with 18–22 MJ/kg due to depletion of high-quality reserves and inflated contracts for low-quality coal (particularly during state capture, 2013–2018). Lower calorific value = more coal burned per MWh = higher effective heat rate.
  • Partial load operation: Due to cycling (frequent starts/stops from load shedding and renewable integration), units operate at partial load where efficiency is 3–8% lower than full load.
  • Age-related degradation: Turbine blading wear, boiler tube fouling, and condenser degradation reduce efficiency by 0.2–0.5% per year past design life.
  • Actual effective heat rate for Eskom's coal fleet in 2024 is estimated at ~11,000–12,500 kJ/kWh on average (compared to ~9,600–10,400 design), meaning actual efficiency is roughly 29–33% vs 35–38% design.
