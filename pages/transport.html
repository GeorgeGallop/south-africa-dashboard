🚦 Department of Transport

Mobility & infrastructure

📑 Overview

The Department of Transport is the national department responsible for the administration of transport policy and infrastructure in South Africa. It oversees road, rail, air and maritime transport and regulates the transport sector.

📄 Functions
  • Developing national transport policy and legislation
  • Coordinating road, rail, air and maritime transport development
  • Regulating public transport and road safety
  • Overseeing the Road Traffic Management Corporation (RTMC)
  • Implementing the National Land Transport Act
  • Managing the construction and maintenance of national transport infrastructure
