📒 Department of Home Affairs

Citizenship & immigration

📑 Overview

The Department of Home Affairs (DHA) is the government department responsible for managing the civil registration of births, marriages and deaths, issuing identity documents and passports, and administering immigration and asylum policy.

📄 Functions
  • Registering births, marriages and deaths
  • Issuing identity documents (IDs) and smart ID cards
  • Issuing passports and travel documents
  • Administering immigration, visas and permits
  • Processing asylum applications and refugee status
  • Determining and certifying citizenship
