Back-hand math
Thermal energy per MWh = 3600 / η (MJ/MWh)
Fuel mass per MWh = (3600 / η) / LHV (kg/MWh)
Fuel cost per MWh = (kg/MWh ÷ 1000) · (fuel_price_usd_per_ton)
Marginal cost ≈ fuel_cost + Variable OPEX per MWh
