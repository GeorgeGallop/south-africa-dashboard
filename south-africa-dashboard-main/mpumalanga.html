Province profile

Mpumalanga

Mpumalanga is a province of coalfields, forests, escarpment scenery, and wildlife tourism, stretching from the Highveld industrial belt to the gateway of Kruger National Park.

At a glance

  • Capital: Mbombela
  • Population: About 4.9 million
  • Largest city: Mbombela
  • Known for: Coal, forestry, and tourism

Population

Mpumalanga has close to 4.9 million residents, with the population concentrated in the Highveld towns, the Lowveld corridor, and communities near the provincial border with Mozambique and Eswatini.

Brief history

Formerly part of the Eastern Transvaal, Mpumalanga has long been shaped by Ndebele, Swazi, and other communities, as well as coal mining and transport links between Gauteng and the eastern Lowveld.

Key industries

Coal mining, electricity generation, forestry, agriculture, food processing, and tourism are central to the provincial economy.

Major cities & towns

Mbombela, Emalahleni, Middelburg, Secunda, Standerton, Ermelo, and Barberton anchor the main economic hubs.

Climate

The province has summer rainfall, with cooler Highveld conditions inland and warmer, wetter Lowveld weather near the escarpment and national parks.

Natural resources

Coal, forestry, fertile cropland, wildlife, rivers, and scenic escarpment landscapes are major resources supporting both industry and tourism.

Future outlook

Mpumalanga’s long-term outlook depends on balancing its coal legacy with renewable energy, tourism, agro-processing, and infrastructure upgrades that support a just transition.
