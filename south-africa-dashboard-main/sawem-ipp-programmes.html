📋 Programme Details

REIPPPP — Renewable Energy Independent Power Producer Procurement Programme

2011–present

The flagship IPP programme. Competitive auctions for onshore wind, solar PV, CSP, small hydro, biomass, landfill gas, and biogas. As of 2023, 123 projects awarded, R256 billion private investment committed, 5% of SA's energy supply. Bid windows were halted between 2015–2019 (blamed for exacerbating load shedding). BW7 (2024) targets 5000 MW as a rolling bid window.

RMIPPPP — Risk Mitigation IPP Procurement Programme

2020

Introduced to quickly bring online ~2000 MW of dispatchable capacity and reduce utilisation of expensive diesel-based OCGT peaking plants. Unlike REIPPPP, RMIPPP is technology-neutral: awarded to gas/diesel reciprocating engines, battery storage, and hybrid projects. 12 preferred bidders selected for 1995 MW total.

BESIPPPP — Battery Energy Storage IPP Procurement Programme

2024–present

Dedicated programme for grid-scale battery storage. BW1 (Nov 2024): 2 projects, 1440 MWh / 360 MW. BW2 (May 2025): 5 projects, 616 MW. BW3 announced May 2025. Helps firm renewable generation and provide fast-responding reserves.
