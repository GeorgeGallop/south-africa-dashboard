SAWEM Trading Platform Framework, 2026

NERSA’s draft rules for the digital market platform — bids, clearing, dispatch, settlement and a five-year ban on fully automated trading.

Open for comment

AuthorityNational Energy Regulator of South Africa (NERSA)
Issued28 September 2026 (Version 1.0)
Public comments due16:00 on 31 October 2026 — send to MSU@nersa.org.za
Energy Council member cut-off27 October 2026
Virtual public hearing19 November 2026, 09:30–12:00 (register by 16:30 on 12 November 2026)

PDFs Framework (Annexure A) · Consultation paper (Annexure B)

What it is

The Framework is the regulatory, governance, operational and technical baseline for the SAWEM trading platform — the software and processes through which participants submit bids and offers, the market is cleared, dispatch schedules are issued and settlement data is exchanged. NERSA published it under the Electricity Regulation Act 2006 as amended by Act 38 of 2024. Approval covers the platform as an integrated ecosystem (systems, algorithms, interfaces, cybersecurity, reporting), whether NTCSA runs the engines in-house or licences third-party software.

What it changes

  • Platform approval — the Market Operator may not go live until NERSA has approved the platform against this Framework. Missing the operational deployment date in that approval can trigger licence variation, suspension or revocation.
  • No algo-trading at launch — fully automated systems that submit bids and offers without manual review are barred from the platform for the first five years after commercial launch. NERSA will review the ban against operating experience; the consultation paper asks whether that freeze is the right transitional measure.
  • Governance — NERSA approves and audits the platform; NTCSA operates it; a ring-fenced market-surveillance unit watches conduct. The Framework is written to sit next to the Market Code, not instead of it.

What to watch

Written comments close 31 October; the hearing is 19 November. Pair this with the still-open EPP 2026 and Market Transformation paper (both due 28 October) and with the Market Code, vesting framework and trading rules still in NERSA consolidation. NTCSA’s SAWEM go-live remains April 2027.
