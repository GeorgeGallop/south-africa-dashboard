What it is

The Market Code is the constitution of SAWEM. It covers who may participate, how the day-ahead market is bid, how the system is balanced in real time, how money is settled, and the role of the Central Purchasing Agency (including vesting contracts). NTCSA holds the market-operator licence; NERSA must approve the Code before the market can go live.

What already happened

Written comments closed 17 June. The Electricity Subcommittee held a public hearing on 1 July 2026. That is the oral-input step. Approval is an Energy Regulator decision and is not on a published agenda.

What to watch

Do not treat Code approval and market go-live as the same date. On 30 September 2026 NTCSA said SAWEM is now scheduled for April 2027, and that it is standing up a ring-fenced market-surveillance unit as a Market Operator licence condition. Vesting, wholesale methodology and trading rules still have to be in force around the Code, not after it.
