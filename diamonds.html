Diamonds

Iconic South African mineral
What are they?

Carbon in its hardest natural crystalline form.

Where are they found?

Kimberley, Cullinan, Venetia, and marine deposits off the west coast.

What are they used for?

Jewellery, industrial cutting tools, high-tech applications.

Supply Chain

Mined → Sorted → Polished → Global jewellery market.
