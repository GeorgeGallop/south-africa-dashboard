♻ Institute of Waste Management of Southern Africa (IWMSA)

Waste governance & practice

📑 Overview

The Institute of Waste Management of Southern Africa (IWMSA) is the professional body representing the waste management sector in South Africa. It promotes best practice in waste management, drives professional development and contributes to waste policy and regulation.

📄 Functions
  • Promoting best practice in the waste management sector
  • Providing professional development, training and certification
  • Advocating for sound waste legislation and policy
  • Facilitating knowledge sharing and networking among practitioners
  • Supporting the transition to a circular economy
  • Contributing to public awareness on waste minimisation
