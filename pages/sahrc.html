🧭 South African Human Rights Commission (SAHRC)

Human rights enforcement

📑 Overview

The South African Human Rights Commission (SAHRC) is the national human rights institution established under Chapter 9 of the Constitution. It promotes, protects and monitors the observance of human rights in South Africa, and investigates violations of the Bill of Rights.

📄 Functions
  • Promoting a culture of human rights through education and advocacy
  • Investigating complaints of human rights violations
  • Monitoring government compliance with the Bill of Rights
  • Conducting public hearings and inquiries on human rights issues
  • Advising government on legislation and policy affecting human rights
  • Reporting to Parliament on the state of human rights
