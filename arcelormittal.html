ArcelorMittal South Africa

The largest steel producer in South Africa and a central owner of smelting, steelmaking and downstream manufacturing assets.

Role in smelting and manufacturing

ArcelorMittal South Africa operates integrated steel mills, blast furnaces and rolling mills. The company smelts iron ore and scrap into liquid steel, then converts it into slabs, beams, sheets and other finished steel products.

Its operations are a core part of South Africa’s metal manufacturing value chain, supplying construction, automotive, mining and industrial customers.

Key manufacturing assets
  • Integrated blast furnace and steelmaking works.
  • Ferroalloy and foundry product production.
  • Hot-rolled and cold-rolled steel plate, coil and section manufacture.
  • Service centres and supply chain support for domestic manufacturing.
Why it matters

ArcelorMittal South Africa links raw materials to finished goods. Its steelmaking and smelting capacity is vital for infrastructure projects, export competitiveness and broader industrial growth.
