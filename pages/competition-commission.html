⚖️ Competition Commission

Competition enforcement

📑 Overview

The Competition Commission is the independent statutory body established under the Competition Act of 1998. It investigates and evaluates restrictive business practices, mergers and acquisitions, and abuse of dominance to promote competition and consumer welfare in South Africa.

📄 Functions
  • Investigating prohibited practices such as price fixing and collusion
  • Reviewing and approving or prohibiting mergers and acquisitions
  • Investigating abuse of dominance by large firms
  • Conducting market inquiries into sectors with competition concerns
  • Referring cases to the Competition Tribunal for adjudication
  • Advising government on competition policy and economic regulation
