🚶 South African Police Service (SAPS)

Public safety & policing

📑 Overview

The South African Police Service (SAPS) is the national police force responsible for maintaining public order, preventing and investigating crime, and protecting the safety and security of all South Africans.

📄 Functions
  • Preventing, combating and investigating crime
  • Maintaining public order and responding to emergencies
  • Protecting the public and their property
  • Enforcing traffic laws and road safety
  • Investigating organised crime, corruption and serious offences
  • Cooperating with other security services
