Coal

Major energy source and export commodity
What is it?

Fossil fuel used primarily for electricity generation and steelmaking.

Where is it found?

Mpumalanga (Witbank, Highveld coalfields) and KwaZulu-Natal.

What is it used for?

~80% of South Africa’s electricity (Eskom), steel production, chemicals, and exports.

Supply Chain

Mined → Transported by rail (Transnet) → Power stations or exported via Richards Bay.
