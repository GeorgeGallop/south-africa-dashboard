Electricity Regulation Amendment Act, 2024

The statute that legalises a competitive wholesale electricity market, an independent transmission/market operator, and NERSA’s market-rule powers.

In force

ActAct No. 38 of 2024
AmendsElectricity Regulation Act 4 of 2006
What it enabledNTCSA, Market Operator licence, SAWEM Market Code, trading rules, vesting, independent TSO
Energy CouncilERA Amendment Act page

What it changed

  • Competitive generation and third-party grid access as the default, not an exception.
  • A licensed Market Operator and a path to an independent, state-owned Transmission System Operator that owns and operates the grid and the market.
  • NERSA duties to approve a Market Code, make trading rules, and oversee vesting/transitional pricing.
  • Section 29 determinations remain the Minister’s security-of-supply tool — now being re-consulted for a market world.

How to use this page

This Act is the parent. Every live item on Bills & Legislation is an implementation instrument. If two documents appear to conflict, the Act wins until NERSA or a court says otherwise.
