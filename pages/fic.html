🔎 Financial Intelligence Centre (FIC)

Anti-money laundering & suspicious activity

📑 Overview

The Financial Intelligence Centre (FIC) is South Africa's financial intelligence unit, established under the Financial Intelligence Centre Act of 2001. It collects, analyses and disseminates financial intelligence to combat money laundering, terrorist financing and associated criminal activities.

📄 Functions
  • Receiving and analysing suspicious transaction reports
  • Monitoring compliance with the Financial Intelligence Centre Act
  • Providing financial intelligence to law enforcement and investigating bodies
  • Maintaining a database of account holders and beneficial owners
  • Cooperating with international financial intelligence units
  • Imposing administrative sanctions on non-compliant institutions
