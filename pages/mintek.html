Mintek

South Africa's national mineral research organisation

Mintek is a state-owned research and technology organisation focused on mineral processing, extractive metallurgy and related fields. Established in 1934 and based in Randburg, it develops and tests technologies that help the minerals industry process and beneficiate South Africa's mineral resources. It is a public research institution, not a mining house operating a portfolio of mines.

Ownership: South African government-owned public research organisation
Established: 1934
Head office: 200 Malibongwe Drive, Randburg, Johannesburg
Core mandate: Research, develop and transfer mineral-processing and metallurgical technologies
Focus: Mineral characterisation, beneficiation, extractive metallurgy, mining technology and advanced materials
Role in the minerals economy: Research partner and technology provider to industry and the public sector

Mineral research

Research, process development and technology transfer across the minerals value chain

What Mintek does

Mineral deposits do not become useful products simply by being mined. Ore must be characterised, separated and processed, and those steps determine what can be recovered, at what cost, and with what environmental footprint. Mintek works on the science and engineering behind those processes, from laboratory research through pilot-scale testing and technology transfer.

  • Mineral processing and characterisation: Tests ore and develops concentration and separation approaches to establish how mineral resources can be beneficiated.
  • Extractive metallurgy: Develops and evaluates methods for recovering metals, including hydrometallurgical and high-temperature processes.
  • Mining, materials and automation: Applies research to mining systems, process control, advanced materials and related technologies.
  • Technology transfer: Works with industry and other partners to move research results toward practical processes, services and products.

Where it fits

Mintek sits in the public research and innovation landscape. Its work supports miners, mineral processors and downstream manufacturers, but Mintek itself is not an ordinary mining company: its central asset is technical capability, research infrastructure and process knowledge. That makes it distinct from state-owned commercial operators such as AEMFC, which mines coal, and from CEF, which holds energy-sector operating businesses.

Its national value is in reducing technical risk and building local capability across the minerals value chain. Research into beneficiation and processing can help unlock value beyond exporting unprocessed ore, while work on automation, materials and cleaner processes connects mineral development to wider industrial and environmental goals.
