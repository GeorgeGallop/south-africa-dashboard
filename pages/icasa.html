📡 ICASA (Communications Regulator)

Broadcasting & telecoms regulation

📑 Overview

The Independent Communications Authority of South Africa (ICASA) is the independent regulator of the broadcasting, telecommunications and postal services sectors. Established under the ICASA Act of 2000, it licenses operators, regulates the radio frequency spectrum and protects consumers.

📄 Functions
  • Issuing broadcasting and telecommunications licences
  • Managing and allocating the radio frequency spectrum
  • Regulating the broadcasting content and code of conduct
  • Promoting competition in the communications sector
  • Protecting consumers from unfair practices and service failures
  • Processing complaints and enforcing compliance
