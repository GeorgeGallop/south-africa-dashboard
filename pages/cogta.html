🏠 Department of Cooperative Governance (CoGTA)

Local government coordination

📑 Overview

The Department of Cooperative Governance and Traditional Affairs (CoGTA) is the national department responsible for overseeing local government and supporting municipalities. It coordinates the relationship between national, provincial and local government and works on traditional affairs.

📄 Functions
  • Overseeing and supporting municipalities to function effectively
  • Coordinating the intergovernmental relations framework
  • Driving municipal capacity building and institutional reform
  • Managing national disaster management coordination
  • Administering traditional affairs and customary leadership
  • Monitoring and responding to municipal financial distress
