Eskom

State-owned electricity utility
Overview

Eskom is South Africa’s primary electricity generator, producing around 95% of the country’s power.

Key Facts
  • Founded: 1923
  • Owner: Government of South Africa (100%)
  • Revenue: ~R 241 billion (2024)
Current Challenges

Load shedding, massive debt, ageing infrastructure, and the transition to renewable energy.
