📊 Statistics South Africa (Stats SA)

Official statistics

📑 Overview

Statistics South Africa (Stats SA) is the national statistical service, established under the Statistics Act of 1999. It produces official statistics on the economy, population and society, and is responsible for conducting the national census and household surveys.

📄 Functions
  • Conducting the national census (every 10 years)
  • Producing official economic statistics (GDP, CPI, employment)
  • Conducting household and labour force surveys
  • Publishing demographic and social statistics
  • Coordinating the national statistical system
  • Providing data to government, business and the public
