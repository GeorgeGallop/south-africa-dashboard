⚡ Department of Electricity and Energy

Electricity, gas & energy policy

📑 Overview

The Department of Electricity and Energy is the national department responsible for the electricity and energy portfolio in South Africa. It was established in 2024 when the former Department of Mineral Resources and Energy (DMRE) was split, with the electricity and energy functions assigned to this department while the mining and petroleum resources portfolio moved to the Department of Mineral and Petroleum Resources.

📄 Functions
  • Developing and implementing national electricity and energy policy
  • Overseeing the electricity sector and the Integrated Resource Plan (IRP)
  • Regulating energy efficiency and renewable energy programmes
  • Managing the gas and petroleum pipeline energy portfolio
  • Coordinating the energy transition and climate-related energy policy
  • Overseeing energy access and electrification programmes
