🤝 CCMA (Dispute Resolution)

Labour relations & arbitration

📑 Overview

The Commission for Conciliation, Mediation and Arbitration (CCMA) is an independent dispute resolution body established under the Labour Relations Act of 1995. It provides conciliation, mediation and arbitration services for disputes between employers, employees and trade unions.

📄 Functions
  • Resolving unfair dismissal and unfair labour practice disputes
  • Providing conciliation and mediation services
  • Conducting arbitration hearings and issuing binding awards
  • Certifying strike and lockout ballots
  • Promoting orderly collective bargaining
  • Providing advice and information on labour law
