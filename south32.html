South32 / Samancor

Owner of major manganese and chrome smelting assets, South32 is a key industrial manager in South Africa’s alloy manufacturing sector.

Role in smelting and alloy manufacturing

South32 controls the Samancor ferrochrome and manganese operations, producing ferroalloys that are essential for steelmaking and high-strength steel.

Its operations focus on converting raw ore into ferrochrome, silicomanganese and other alloy products through submerged arc furnace smelting technology.

Key assets
  • Ferrochrome plants in Rustenburg and Brits.
  • Manganese alloy production facilities.
  • Supply chains serving steel mills in South Africa and abroad.
Why it matters

South32’s manufacturing presence is centered on alloy production and raw material conversion, making it a strategic supplier for downstream steel and metal manufacturing industries.
