🤖 Agent Framework — Full Feature Engineering

Every agent in the SAWEM ABM with its fully broken-out state space, action space, policy function, outcome variables, and metadata.

The model is a two-timescale architecture: the Power Market ABM runs at hourly resolution (OPF clearing every hour, agents learning on daily/ weekly reward signals), while the Macro Economy ABM runs at 6-month to annual resolution (capital investment, household adoption, industrial growth, Eskom corporate restructuring). The macro states define demand scenarios that shape the hourly market outcomes, which in turn aggregate into economic indicators that feed back into macro decisions.

⏰ Timescale Key: Hourly Daily Weekly 6-month / Quarterly Annual

⚡️ Power Market Agents Hourly OPF cycle

Bid into the Day-Ahead Market, settled at SMP, learn from hourly/ daily profit signals.

🏦 Central Purchasing Agency (CPA) Daily DAM trading Annual legacy charge reset
DimensionVariablesDescription
Statelegacy_account_balance (R), vesting_contracts[station][hedge_pct, avail_payment, cap_payment, strike_price], legacy_ipp_ppas[project][price,volume], smp_forecast_annual, total_load (MWh) Legacy account: accumulates surplus/deficit from vesting + legacy IPP trading. Vesting contract to Eskom Gen: pays availability (R/year) + capacity (R/kW/month) + energy hedge. Energy hedge = hedge_pct × strike + (1−hedge_pct) × SMP. hedge_pct declines 100% → 0% over 5 years.
Actiontrade_vesting_portfolio_into_dam (submit generation schedule for all Eskom stations and legacy IPPs), set_sales_agreement_price (NERSA-approved), procure_ancillary_services, apply_legacy_charge (R/MWh) CPA registers as BRP for ~70% of system generation in yr1. Trades all vesting/legacy energy into DAM as price-taker (must-offer). Legacy charge = (Legacy Account deficit) / (total load MWh) — recovered from consumers.
Policyπ: balance legacy account — if deficit grows, increase legacy charge; if surplus, decrease. CPA does not strategically bid — it must run all contracted energy. CPA is not a profit maximiser — it is a pass-through mechanism. Its only "policy" is legacy charge smoothing (avoid volatility for consumers). Market power mitigation: the vesting hedge ensures Eskom Gen cannot exercise full market power during transition.
Outcomelegacy_charge_actual (R/MWh), cpa_financial_position (R), transition_effectiveness (market_power_mitigation %) Low SMP → CPA deficit → higher legacy charge → consumers pay. High SMP → CPA surplus → negative legacy charge (consumers receive credit). Market power mitigation: compare SMP in model with vs without vesting hedge.

🔃 Two-Timescale Integration: How the Layers Connect

The macro economy agents run at annual resolution and produce the demand growth scenario:

Macro_Statet+1 = f(Macro_Statet, Eskom_Corp_Statet, Policy_Statet, Hourly_OPF_Outcomest−12m..t)

Specifically, the macro-to-power-market mapping is:

Macro → Hourly Load Shape
GDP_Δ% + population_Δ% + electrification_Δ% + sector_shifts + weather → annual TWh
Annual TWh + smart_meter_profiles + TOU_coefficients → 8760 hourly load vector Dh
Hourly OPF → Annual Economic Indicators
Σ(SMPh × Dh) / ΣDh = avg_wholesale_price (R/MWh)

The power market ABM runs hourly for 365 days within each macro year, then aggregates outcomes to inform the macro agents' next annual state update. This means a 20-year simulation involves: 20 × (365 × 24 = 8,760) = 175,200 OPF solves, each with agent learning updates at daily/weekly frequency. The MADDPG multi-agent learning adjusts generator bidding policies on the first-of-month reset so agents adapt to changing fuel prices, carbon tax, and competitor behaviour over the simulation.
