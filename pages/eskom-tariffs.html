🧾 What's Actually in Your Electricity Bill?

A single c/kWh rate hides a lot of complexity. Eskom's tariff bundles 10+ separate charges. For a typical large customer, the Active Energy Charge makes up about 60-70% of the total — the rest is fixed and capacity charges you pay regardless of how much power you use.

⚡ Active Energy Charge — WHAT YOU CONSUME

Unit: c/kWh · Like paying for litres of water used. Price depends on when (Peak/Std/Off-Pk), season (winter = more), voltage (higher = cheaper), and distance. This is 60-70% of your total bill.

🏗️ Active Demand Charge — HOW BIG YOUR CONNECTION IS

Unit: R/kVA/month · Like renting a water pipe regardless of flow. You declare a Notified Maximum Demand (NMD) and pay monthly whether you use it or not. Exceed it → 3× penalty. Sub-components: Gen Capacity (R 9.40), TUoS (R 48.10), DUoS (R 12.50) per kVA/m.

🏢 Non-Local Authority — Eskom → You

Eskom sells directly. Rates set by NERSA nationally — same everywhere. Typically 15-30% cheaper than municipal. All charges shown in this dashboard are Non-Local rates.

🏛️ Local Authority — Eskom → Municipality → You

Municipality buys bulk from Eskom and resells. Rates set by municipal council — Cape Town, Joburg, Durban have different prices. Municipality adds surcharges: distribution fees, cross-subsidies, own infrastructure costs.

💡 Bottom line: The Active Energy Charge is the headline number, but Legacy Charge (0.2278 R/kWh), Generation Capacity (R 9.40/kVA), and Network Charges (TUoS + DUoS = R 60.60/kVA) add significant fixed costs. The "c/kWh you see" is only part of the story.
