Phase 2 - Local Government and Beyond

The second wave of reform, announced May 2025 - expanding Operation Vulindlela beyond network industries into local government, water services, metropolitan rail, spatial integration and digital government.

Why Phase 2 was needed

Phase 1 proved the delivery model. But the biggest constraints on the lived experience of citizens - failing municipalities, collapsing water services, broken commuter rail and apartheid spatial legacy - sit outside the original five focus areas. Phase 2 extends the same model to them while continuing the core energy, logistics and visa reforms.

What has happened so far
  • Local government: The White Paper on Local Government was finalised; the MFMA Amendment Bill drafted; the Public Service Commission's mandate extended to municipal oversight; the Metro Trading Services Reform Programme launched to fix electricity, water and waste trading in metros.
  • Water: WATERCOM operationalised and driving municipal water interventions; Water Services Amendment Bill advanced.
  • Digital: Digital public infrastructure implementation accelerated (MyMzansi, MzansiXchange).
  • Freight/rail (continuing): The freight logistics reforms continue, including PRASA alignment and rail open access.
Where it stands now (2026)

Phase 2 is in early-to-mid implementation. Water and digital are the strongest performers; spatial integration and housing lag. The municipal reform agenda (MFMA amendments, metro trading services) is the most politically sensitive and the key test of Phase 2.

What is projected
  • Financially and operationally sustainable metros through the Trading Services Reform Programme.
  • Stronger municipal audit outcomes and accountability via the expanded PSC mandate and MFMA amendments.
  • Improved water services delivery in municipalities as WATERCOM interventions scale.
  • More integrated, densified cities that lower the cost of living and unlock urban economic potential.
  • Government services delivered digitally through MyMzansi/MzansiXchange.
