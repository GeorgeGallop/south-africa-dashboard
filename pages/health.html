🩺 Department of Health

Public health services

📑 Overview

The Department of Health is the national department responsible for the administration of public health in South Africa. It sets national health policy, manages the public health system and drives the implementation of the National Health Insurance (NHI).

📄 Functions
  • Developing national health policy and legislation
  • Managing the public health care system
  • Coordinating the response to disease outbreaks and pandemics
  • Implementing the National Health Insurance (NHI) reforms
  • Regulating health care facilities, medicines and services
  • Promoting public health and disease prevention
