⚔️ Department of Mineral and Petroleum Resources (DMPR)

Mining, minerals & petroleum resources

📑 Overview

The Department of Mineral and Petroleum Resources (DMPR) is the national department responsible for regulating South Africa's mining, minerals and petroleum resources. It was established in 2024 when the former Department of Mineral Resources and Energy (DMRE) was split, with the mining and petroleum portfolio assigned to the DMPR and the electricity and energy portfolio moving to the Department of Electricity and Energy.

📄 Functions
  • Administering the Mineral and Petroleum Resources Development Act (MPRDA)
  • Issuing and regulating prospecting, mining and production rights
  • Promoting investment in the mining and petroleum sectors
  • Overseeing the extraction of strategic minerals and petroleum resources
  • Promoting mineral beneficiation and local value addition
  • Coordinating mine safety, health and environmental compliance
