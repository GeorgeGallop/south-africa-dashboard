🧾 National Credit Regulator (NCR)

Credit regulation

📑 Overview

The National Credit Regulator (NCR) is the independent regulator of the South African credit industry, established under the National Credit Act of 2005. It regulates credit providers, credit bureaus and debt counsellors, and protects consumers from reckless lending and unfair credit practices.

📄 Functions
  • Registering credit providers, credit bureaus and payment distribution agents
  • Monitoring compliance with the National Credit Act
  • Investigating complaints against credit providers
  • Promoting responsible lending and fair credit practices
  • Advising the Minister of Trade and Industry on credit policy
  • Conducting research on the state of the consumer credit market
