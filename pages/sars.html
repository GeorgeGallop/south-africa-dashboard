🧾 South African Revenue Service (SARS)

Tax administration

📑 Overview

The South African Revenue Service (SARS) is the revenue authority of South Africa, established under the SARS Act of 1997. It collects all national taxes, customs and excise duties, and administers the country's tax and customs legislation efficiently and fairly.

📄 Functions
  • Collecting personal, corporate and VAT taxes
  • Administering customs and excise duties
  • Processing tax returns and issuing refunds
  • Implementing the eFiling system for taxpayer services
  • Combating tax evasion, fraud and non-compliance
  • Facilitating legitimate international trade through customs control
