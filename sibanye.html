Sibanye-Stillwater

A major PGM and gold group with smelting, refining and manufacturing links in South Africa.

Role in smelting and downstream processing

Sibanye-Stillwater operates smelting and refining facilities that process platinum group metal concentrates into marketable metals and chemicals.

Its downstream capabilities support the broader manufacturing value chain for automotive catalysts, industrial chemicals and specialist metal products.

Key activities
  • PGM smelting and refining operations.
  • Gold refining and precious metal production.
  • Delivery of feedstock into manufacturing sectors such as automotive and electronics.
Why it matters

Sibanye-Stillwater’s processing footprint helps keep more value in South Africa by converting mined output into refined metals that are essential to global manufacturing.
