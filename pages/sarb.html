🏦 South African Reserve Bank (SARB)

Monetary stability & policy

📑 Overview

The South African Reserve Bank (SARB) is South Africa's central bank, established in 1921 under the Currency and Banking Act. It is responsible for monetary policy, financial stability, currency issuance and the management of the country's gold and foreign exchange reserves.

📄 Functions
  • Setting and implementing monetary policy (inflation targeting)
  • Issuing banknotes and coins
  • Acting as banker to the government and commercial banks
  • Managing the national payment system
  • Maintaining financial stability and regulating the banking system
  • Managing gold and foreign exchange reserves
