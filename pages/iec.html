🙃 Electoral Commission (IEC)

Elections & electoral integrity

📑 Overview

The Electoral Commission of South Africa (IEC) is the independent constitutional body responsible for managing free, fair and credible elections at national, provincial and municipal level. Established under the Electoral Commission Act of 1996, it is one of the Chapter 9 institutions established to strengthen constitutional democracy.

📄 Functions
  • Compiling and maintaining the national voters' roll
  • Managing, conducting and supervising national, provincial and municipal elections
  • Registering political parties and ensuring compliance with electoral law
  • Delimiting voting districts and appointing election officials
  • Conducting voter education and promoting electoral participation
  • Certifying election results and declaring outcomes
