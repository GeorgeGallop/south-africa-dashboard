Wholesale Electricity Pricing Methodology

The recipe that turns allowed generation revenue into a wholesale energy price, a capacity charge and a legacy charge.

What it is

In a competitive market you still need a regulated wholesale tariff for the part of the system that is not yet fully merchant: Eskom Generation during the transition, legacy IPPs, and residual supply to distributors. This methodology unbundles that tariff.

The three building blocks

  • Wholesale energy (R/MWh) — time-of-use, seasonal. Recovers generation costs not picked up by the other two charges.
  • Generation capacity charge — pays for keeping plant available.
  • Legacy charge — section 34 PPA costs, stranded-asset or subsidy amounts that should not sit in the competitive energy price.

NERSA illustrated the idea with a R250 billion MYPD 6 generation-revenue split across TOU periods. Stakeholders were asked whether that allocation is the right one.

What to watch

The 14 August hearing was the oral-input step, not the decision. Pair this page with the transitional generation pricing and vesting framework (the hedge that sits on top of these prices) and EPP 2026 (the policy that will govern the next methodology review).
