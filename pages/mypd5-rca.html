What an RCA is

MYPD sets allowed revenue in advance. Reality diverges — diesel/OCGT burn, sales volumes, primary energy. The RCA measures the variance for a closed financial year. NERSA then decides how much of that variance is efficient and may be clawed back from (or given back to) customers over subsequent years.

Why this one matters

FY2024/25 is the last MYPD5 year. The outcome feeds the tariff path into MYPD6/7 and into the 10-year price forecast the new EPP 2026 will require. It is a revenue decision, not a market-design decision, but it moves the same customer bill as vesting and wholesale methodology.

What to watch
