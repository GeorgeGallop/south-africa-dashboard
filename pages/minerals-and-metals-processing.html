Minerals & Metals Processing

The manufacturing chain that transforms mined ore into concentrate, metal, alloys, and refined products for industry and export.

What this covers

Minerals and metals processing is the largest manufacturing domain in South Africa's industrial base. It starts where mining ends — taking run-of-mine ore and upgrading it through concentration, smelting, refining, and alloying into the metals and materials that supply steelmaking, automotive, construction, electronics, and export markets.

This chain is responsible for the bulk of South Africa's mineral value-add before export and is the foundation of the country's metals-based manufacturing competitiveness.

Concentration

Upgrading run-of-mine ore into high-grade concentrate through crushing, grinding, flotation, gravity and magnetic separation. The essential first step that makes smelting economically viable.

Smelting

High-temperature extraction of metal from concentrate using furnaces, reductants and fluxes. Produces the crude metal or matte feedstock for refining and alloying.

The full process chain
  1. Mining — ore extracted from the ground (covered under the Mining section).
  2. Concentration — ore crushed, milled, and upgraded to concentrate. →
  3. Smelting — concentrate heated to separate metal from slag. →
  4. Refining and purification — producing commercially pure metals and chemical intermediates.
  5. Alloying, steel and ferroalloy production — blending metals for steel, stainless steel and specialty alloys.

Downstream customers include the steel industry, automotive manufacturing, construction, electrical equipment, and export commodity markets.
