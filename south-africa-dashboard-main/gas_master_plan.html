Gas Master Plan

The South African Gas Master Plan outlines the policy, infrastructure and market steps needed to turn gas into a reliable part of the energy mix.

Strategic roadmap

The 2024 Gas Master Plan is designed to coordinate gas supply, transport and demand development across the country. It identifies priority corridors, demand nodes, and the infrastructure sequencing required to make gas work for power, industry and export-linked markets.

The plan is a policy blueprint, not an operational project list. It aims to create the market conditions needed for investors to build pipelines, LNG terminals, and gas-fired generation while protecting energy security.

Key high points
  • Priority demand nodes are mapped for coastal ports, industrial clusters and power generation sites, making gas corridor planning more targeted.
  • Domestic onshore and offshore gas resources are integrated with LNG import terminals, meaning local supply and imports must work together.
  • Investment sequencing focuses on high-impact corridors first, then inland links, to avoid building stranded assets too early.
  • Market reforms are proposed to support third-party access, transparent tariffs and private-sector participation in gas trading.
  • The plan explicitly ties gas development to hydrogen and carbon management strategies, showing gas as part of a broader transition package.

Implementation focus
  • Gas imports and regasification are treated as the near-term anchor for supply while local resources are developed.
  • Priority corridors are selected to link coastal terminals with inland industrial and power demand.
  • Infrastructure planning is calibrated to avoid overbuilding before demand is established.
  • Regulation is intended to increase transparency and lower investment barriers.
