Market Inquiry: Fixed & Generation Capacity Charges

NERSA’s draft inquiry into fixed charges and unbundled generation-capacity charges levied by municipal distributors and Eskom Distribution.

Input consolidation

AuthorityNERSA
Issued25 June 2026
Comments closed27 July 2026
Energy CouncilDraft inquiry report

What it is

As Eskom unbundles generation charges, municipalities have copied fixed and capacity charges onto retail bills. Industry says the combination with remaining energy charges is crushing smelters and factories. NERSA opened a market inquiry. The June 2026 draft found that negotiated price agreements have not given the industrial base adequate relief.

Why it matters

This is the distribution-side twin of EPP 2026. You can write a beautiful wholesale methodology and still lose ferrochrome if municipal fixed charges recreate the old bundled price. The inquiry is NERSA looking at that leakage.

What to watch

Comments closed 27 July. Still in consolidation. Final inquiry findings would constrain how distributors may structure fixed and capacity charges under the new EPP.
