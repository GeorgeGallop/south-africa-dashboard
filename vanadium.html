Vanadium

World #1 producer
What is it?

Hard, silvery-grey metal used in high-strength steel and energy storage.

Where is it found?

Bushveld Complex (same geological formation as PGMs).

What is it used for?

High-strength steel and vanadium redox flow batteries.

Supply Chain

Often co-produced with PGMs → Refined for steel and battery markets.
