Impala Platinum

A leading producer of platinum group metals with integrated smelting and refining operations in South Africa.

Role in smelting and refining

Impala Platinum operates smelters and refineries that convert PGM concentrates into matte and then refined metal products such as platinum, palladium and rhodium.

Its manufacturing contribution is in producing high-value metal intermediates and finished precious metal products for global markets.

Key assets
  • Integrated smelters and refineries in Rustenburg.
  • Matte conversion and base metal separation facilities.
  • PGM product streams for automotive, industrial and investment markets.
Why it matters

Impala Platinum is central to South Africa’s downstream precious metals sector, turning mined concentrates into refined metals that are exported or used in global manufacturing.
