🏫 Department of Trade, Industry & Competition (DTIC)

Trade & industrial policy

📑 Overview

The Department of Trade, Industry and Competition (the dtic) is the national department responsible for industrial policy, trade policy and economic regulation. It promotes the competitiveness of South African industry and facilitates investment and export growth.

📄 Functions
  • Developing and implementing industrial policy
  • Regulating international trade and facilitating exports
  • Administering the Competition Act and consumer protection
  • Promoting Black Economic Empowerment (B-BBEE) and transformation
  • Attracting foreign direct investment
  • Supporting small business development and enterprise growth
