🧕 Public Service Commission (PSC)

Public service oversight

📑 Overview

The Public Service Commission (PSC) is an independent constitutional body established under Chapter 10 of the Constitution. It promotes the values and principles of public administration and oversees the performance of the public service and its compliance with the Constitution and law.

📄 Functions
  • Promoting a professional, ethical and accountable public service
  • Investigating grievances and complaints about public administration
  • Advising on the recruitment, appointment and promotion of public servants
  • Monitoring and evaluating the performance of departments
  • Investigating maladministration and misconduct in the public service
  • Reporting to Parliament on the state of the public service
