Back-hand math
Waste energy (annual): E_waste = t/y · 1000 kg/t · LHV (MJ/kg)
Electricity (annual): E_elec = E_waste · η · 0.277777… (kWh per MJ)
Capacity (avg): P ≈ E_elec / (8760 h)
Example default: 2,500 per (t/y) — adjust.
