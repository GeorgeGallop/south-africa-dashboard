⚖️ Department of Justice & Constitutional Development

Justice system governance

📑 Overview

The Department of Justice and Constitutional Development (DoJ&CD) is the government department responsible for the administration of justice, the provision of legal services to the state, and the protection of constitutional and legal rights.

📄 Functions
  • Administering the courts and the judicial system
  • Providing legal services to the state
  • Administering the Master of the High Court and the Deeds Office
  • Implementing the Promotion of Access to Information Act
  • Overseeing the transformation of the justice system
  • Promoting the rule of law and constitutional compliance
