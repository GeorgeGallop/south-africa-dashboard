🏗️ Top 12 Priority Infrastructure Projects

The 12 flagship infrastructure projects receiving project preparation support from Infrastructure South Africa (ISA) — the state's centre of excellence for infrastructure planning, management and delivery, reporting to the Presidential Infrastructure Coordinating Commission (PICC).

About Infrastructure South Africa

Infrastructure South Africa has been the country's coordinating body for infrastructure since 2020. It acts as the single point of entry for infrastructure projects — providing project preparation funding, technical support and bankable business case development for priority government and state-owned company (SOC) projects.

The Top 12 list was showcased at the Sustainable Infrastructure Development Symposium of South Africa (SIDSSA) — ISA's flagship annual event. At SIDSSA 2025 (26–27 May 2025, Century City Convention Centre, Cape Town), ISA:

  • Launched the second edition of the Construction Book — 250+ projects valued at over R268 billion, nearly double the value of the previous edition.
  • Announced the Top 12 priority projects selected for project preparation support.
  • Named the top seven catalytic projects to benefit from a first-of-its-kind R600 million funding allocation to accelerate implementation.

The Top 12 span energy, water, transport, education, healthcare and industrial development — from gas-to-power terminals in Ngqura and Richards Bay, to a 1,500 MW pumped storage scheme in Limpopo, the Durban Container Terminal expansion, and the reinstatement of the Mossel Bay refinery.

The 12 Priority Projects

#1 National
🏥 Healthcare Infrastructure Programme

Accelerating the maintenance, refurbishment and upgrades of health facilities to comply with national health standards — supporting the delivery of high-quality, accessible healthcare and increased life expectancy as envisaged in the NDP.

Project value: TBC (on conclusion of conditional assessments)

ISA support: Funding for conditional assessments to enable a bankable business case.

#2 Limpopo, KZN, NC, EC
🎓 Education Infrastructure Programme

Eradicating schools with asbestos roofs and improper structures damaged by weather events to provide a conducive learning environment — covering schools across Limpopo, KwaZulu-Natal, Northern Cape and Eastern Cape.

Project value: R15.6 bn (LP & KZN schools) · R9.1 bn (NC & EC schools)

ISA support: Conditional assessments (LP & KZN) and pipeline support (NC & EC) toward bankable business cases.

#3 Eastern Cape · Gqeberha
⛽ Ngqura Port LNG Import Terminal

The Port of Ngqura is one of three ports identified as suitable for LNG importation. As part of the Gas to Power Programme, TNPA will develop port infrastructure (marine and landside) for an LNG Import Terminal.

Project value: R2.1 bn

ISA support: Pre-feasibility study, feasibility studies and environmental authorisations.

#4 Gauteng – Eastern Cape
🚂 Project Ukuvuselela (High-Capacity Rail)

A high-capacity rail corridor between Gauteng and the Eastern Cape built for the automotive sector — shifting vehicle exports from road to rail and unlocking the Eastern Cape's export logistics capacity.

Project value: R7 bn

ISA support: Development of a bankable business case.

#5 Eastern Cape
💧 Amatola Bulk Water Augmentation

Upgrade of five (5) Water Treatment Plants to increase the provision of bulk water supply to communities in the Amatola supply area — securing water for households and economic activity.

Project value: R6.83 bn

ISA support: Development of a bankable business case.

#6 Mpumalanga
🏭 Nkomazi Special Economic Zone

A multi-sectoral industrial zone designed to act as a powerful magnet for primary agricultural production — attracting agri-processing, manufacturing and logistics investment to the Nkomazi region.

Project value: R8.4 bn

ISA support: Development of a feasibility study.

#7 Northern Cape
⚙️ Namakwa Special Economic Zone

The NAMSEZ (Northern Cape's first SEZ) includes mineral beneficiation, manufacturing and agro-processing, and will explore value-add services to these sectors — anchored in the region's mining and renewable energy corridors.

Project value: R3.5 bn

ISA support: Feasibility study and bankable business case.

#8 KwaZulu-Natal · Richards Bay
🔥 LNG Import Terminal (Richards Bay)

Construction of a 170,000m³ Floating Storage and Regasification Unit (FSRU) and 170,000m³ Floating Storage Unit (FSU), with an onshore LNG terminal including regasification facility and 440,000m³ of onshore LNG storage at the South Dunes Precinct.

Project value: R2.7 bn

ISA support: Specialist studies and engineering designs for the FSRU.

#9 KwaZulu-Natal · Durban
🚢 Durban Container Terminal (DCT) Pier 1

Pier 1 Phase 1 expansion to increase container capacity — infilling between DCT Pier 1 and Salisbury Island and constructing 5 new deep-water berths to accommodate the larger vessels now calling the terminal.

Project value: R38 bn

ISA support: Navy relocation coordination and feasibility study.

#10 Limpopo
⚡ Eskom Tubatse Pumped Storage Scheme

Construction of a 1,500 MW pumped storage hydropower facility in Limpopo — a large-scale grid-stability asset that stores energy when demand is low and releases it at peak times.

Project value: R35.9 bn

ISA support: Engineering studies and Environmental & Social Impact Assessment for a bankable business case.

#11 Gauteng · Tshwane
🧻 Rooiwal Wastewater Treatment Works Phase 2

Construction of an 80 ML/d extension to the existing plant and an extension to the sludge facility — enabling improved, reliable water and sanitation services for the Rooiwal catchment.

Project value: R1.45 bn

ISA support: Development of a bankable business case.

#12 Western Cape · Mossel Bay
🛢️ Reinstatement of Mossel Bay GTL Refinery

Reinstatement of the PetroSA Mossel Bay operations — the FA Platform and the Gas-to-Liquids (GTL) Refinery — restoring domestic liquid fuels production and jobs on the Southern Cape coast.

Project value: R4.3 bn

ISA support: Specialist studies and feasibility study funding (FA platform & GTL).

Why these projects matter
  • Bankable pipeline: ISA's project preparation role converts concept proposals into feasibility-tested, bankable business cases that can attract public and private capital.
  • Catalytic funding: The R600 million allocation for the top seven catalytic projects is the first of its kind, de-risking early-stage development work.
  • Growth and jobs: Combined committed project values exceed R135 billion (excluding the TBC healthcare programme), spanning construction, energy security, logistics, education and healthcare.
  • Energy security: Gas-to-power (Ngqura, Richards Bay) and grid storage (Tubatse) directly support the structural fix for load shedding.
  • Export competitiveness: DCT Pier 1 and the Ukuvuselela rail corridor relieve port and rail bottlenecks that have constrained exports.
