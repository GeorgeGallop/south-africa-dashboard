Province profile

Gauteng

Gauteng is South Africa’s smallest province by area but the country’s economic powerhouse, anchored by Johannesburg, Tshwane, and Ekurhuleni in the heart of the Witwatersrand urban corridor.

At a glance

  • Capital: Johannesburg / Pretoria
  • Population: About 16.2 million
  • Largest city: Johannesburg
  • Known for: Finance, commerce, and innovation

Population

Gauteng holds about 16.2 million residents, making it the most densely populated province in South Africa. Its growth is driven by migration, job opportunities, and urban expansion.

Brief history

The province grew out of the Witwatersrand gold rush, industrial expansion, and the political history of Pretoria and Johannesburg. It remains central to South Africa’s national economy and governance.

Key industries

Finance, banking, ICT, manufacturing, logistics, retail, business services, and automotive production dominate the provincial economy.

Major cities & towns

Johannesburg, Pretoria, Ekurhuleni, Soweto, Midrand, Vereeniging, and Vanderbijlpark form the province’s main urban and industrial centres.

Climate

Gauteng has a highveld climate with warm, rainy summers and cool, dry winters. Thunderstorms are common in summer, while winter days are usually sunny and crisp.

Natural resources

Gold, building materials, dolomite, water infrastructure, and a highly skilled labour pool support the province’s industrial base.

Future outlook

Gauteng is positioned for growth in smart mobility, digital services, green construction, advanced manufacturing, and regional trade—provided infrastructure and urban service delivery keep pace with demand.
