Province profile

North West

North West is a mineral-rich province on the edge of the Gauteng economy, known for platinum mining, agricultural production, and major tourism destinations such as Sun City and the Pilanesberg.

At a glance

  • Capital: Mahikeng
  • Population: About 4.0 million
  • Largest city: Rustenburg
  • Known for: Platinum, farming, and resorts

Population

The province is home to about 4.0 million people, with growth concentrated around mining towns, district service centres, and the agricultural belt close to the Gauteng border.

Brief history

North West sits on the historical lands of Tswana communities and later became known for frontier politics, bantustan-era administration, and the rise of platinum mining in the modern era.

Key industries

Mining, especially platinum group metals, agriculture, manufacturing, tourism, and retail support the provincial economy.

Major cities & towns

Mahikeng, Rustenburg, Klerksdorp, Potchefstroom, Brits, Vryburg, and Lichtenburg are the main urban centres.

Climate

North West has a mostly semi-arid to savanna climate with hot summers, cool winters, and summer thunderstorms. Rainfall is variable but generally lower than in the eastern provinces.

Natural resources

Platinum, gold, chromium, rich farmland, grazing land, and wildlife reserves make the province both mineral and agricultural in character.

Future outlook

Future gains will likely come from better mine beneficiation, irrigation efficiency, tourism growth, and infrastructure that links rural producers to Gauteng’s larger market.
