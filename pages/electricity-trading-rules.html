Draft NERSA Rules on Electricity Trading

The licence-and-conduct rules for who may buy and sell electricity, and how retail competition is phased in.

Open — deadline extended

AuthorityNERSA
Issued26 June 2026 (draft rules version 3 dated May 2026)
Original due date29 July 2026
Extended due date28 September 2026
Legal hookElectricity Regulation Act ss 35(1) and 35(3)(c), (d), (k)
Energy CouncilConsultation page + extension notice

What it is

Once generation is competitive, someone still has to retail, wheel, and settle. These rules say who may hold a trading licence, how traders relate to distributors and to SAWEM, and how retail competition is switched on in phases.

Phasing

  • Phases 1–2 are in the body of the draft: licensed traders as counterparties, SAWEM participation, PPAs, contracts-for-differences, secondary trading.
  • Phases 3–4 (customer switching and fuller retail contestability) sit in appendices and will be filled in later.
  • When NERSA approves the Market Code, the Code prevails over these rules for SAWEM participants. NERSA then has 12 months to gazette which trading-rule clauses migrate, change or fall away.

What to watch

This is the last big NERSA SAWEM instrument still open. The extension notice is on the Energy Council page. After 28 September it should move to input consolidation; there is no hearing date announced for the extended round.
