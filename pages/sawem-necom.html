🔍 What is NECOM?

NECOM is the National Energy Crisis Committee, a multi-stakeholder body chaired by the Minister of Electricity and reporting to the President. It was formed in direct response to the worst load shedding in South Africa's history (over 200 days in 2022). Its mandate: coordinate, accelerate, and track the implementation of the Energy Action Plan (EAP), the government's primary strategy to resolve the electricity crisis.

NECOM replaced the older National Energy Crisis Committee structure and operates with dedicated workstreams covering: generation recovery, new capacity procurement, private generation enablement, transmission expansion, sector reform, and energy efficiency. The committee meets weekly and publishes Ministerial updates on generation performance, key milestones, and load shedding outlook.

🧱 The Five Pillars of the Energy Action Plan

1️⃣ Fix Eskom and improve existing supply

Eskom is fixing the six power stations that cause the most load shedding. Kusile's SO2 bypass stacks (units 1–3 restored in 2024, +2 000 MW capacity) and Medupi recovery plans are prioritised. Government provided R254 billion debt relief to Eskom so it can buy diesel for OCGTs, invest in maintenance, and expand the transmission network. The Distribution Demand Management Programme (DDMP) pays qualifying businesses to reduce usage. Target: +3 000 MW available capacity by 2024.

2️⃣ Buy as much new power as possible

An additional 400 MW has been imported from Cahora Bassa (Mozambique), with plans for more from neighbouring countries. Government approved procurement of new wind, solar, and battery storage totalling over 14 000 MW from IPPs — including REIPPPP BW6 (2 600 MW), BW7 (5 000 MW), BESIPPPP (battery storage), and RMIPPPP (dispatchable capacity).

3️⃣ Unleash businesses and households to invest in rooftop solar

Tax incentives: businesses can deduct 125% of the cost of solar PV (minimum 1 MW) from taxable income in the first year; households get a rebate of 25% of the cost of new solar panels (up to R15 000). The bounce-back loan scheme helps SMEs go solar. Surplus power can now be sold back to the grid (net metering) in many areas. Result: over 5 500 MW of rooftop solar installed by mid-2024.

4️⃣ Enable private investment in generation

Schedule 2 of the Electricity Regulation Act amended — generation projects up to 100 MW no longer require a generation licence (removed entirely in ERA Amendment Act), unlocking a massive pipeline. Eskom's Standard Offer Programme buys power from surplus generators for 3 years. Environmental permits for transmission infrastructure waived in low-impact areas; permits issued in 57 days for Strategic Infrastructure Projects; NERSA registration takes 19 days. Grid connections now take 6 months. 25 priority transmission projects will add 12 GW capacity by 2028.

5️⃣ Transform the electricity sector

The National Transmission Company of SA (NTCSA) has been established as an independent TSO and Market Operator. The Electricity Regulation Amendment Act 38 of 2024 provides for a competitive wholesale market (SAWEM) with market codes, rules, and a Central Purchasing Agency. In the future, consumers can choose their electricity supplier. Eskom's unbundling into separate generation, transmission, and distribution entities is the structural foundation for the new market.

🏛️ NECOM Workstreams

  • Generation Recovery — Eskom plant performance, maintenance optimisation, Kusile/Medupi recovery
  • New Capacity Procurement — REIPPPP, BESIPPPP, RMIPPPP, gas-to-power, LNG
  • Private Generation — Schedule II reform, Standard Offer, Emergency Generation Programme
  • Transmission Expansion — 25 priority projects, grid connection queue reform, land-use authorisation
  • Energy Efficiency & Demand Management — DDMP, rooftop solar incentives, public lighting efficiency
  • Sector Reform — ERA Amendment Act implementation, NTCSA establishment, market code development
  • Fuel Security — diesel supply for OCGTs, coal stockpile management, gas supply agreements

  • 7 500 MW+ rooftop solar installed by households & businesses
  • ERA Amendment Act 38/2024 signed into law
  • NTCSA established as independent TSO

  • BESIPPPP BW1 & BW2 — 976 MW / 2 144 MWh battery storage awarded
