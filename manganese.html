Manganese

World #1 reserves and producer
What is it?

Essential alloying metal for steel and increasingly important for batteries.

Where is it found?

Kalahari Manganese Field (Northern Cape) — the largest deposit on Earth.

What is it used for?

Steel production (90%+), EV battery cathodes, fertilizers.

Supply Chain

Mined → Processed into ferro-manganese → Exported to global steel mills.
