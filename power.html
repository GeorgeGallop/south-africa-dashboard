Transmission Development Plan 2025-2034

The official National Transmission Company South Africa (NTCSA) plan for long-term transmission network expansion and reinforcement.

The TDP 2025-2034 identifies the high-voltage transmission investments needed to support South Africa's growing demand, improve security of supply, and unlock new generation capacity from renewable energy zones, independent power producers, and cross-border imports.

This plan lays out the proposed bulk network upgrades, new 400 kV and 765 kV corridors, substation expansions, and network reinforcements needed to balance load growth, ageing infrastructure, and the transition away from coal. It also addresses system stability, congestion relief, and the phased delivery of transmission projects over the next decade.

Key features include:

  • Network reinforcement for renewable integration and large-scale solar and wind projects
  • New transmission corridors to connect generation clusters to demand centres
  • Strengthening of existing transmission assets to reduce outages and improve reliability
  • Provisioning for future distributed and embedded generation
  • Planning for cross-border capacity and regional power trade

The TDP acts as the transmission counterpart to the Integrated Resource Plan, ensuring that generation ambitions are matched by a robust grid capable of delivering power across the country.
