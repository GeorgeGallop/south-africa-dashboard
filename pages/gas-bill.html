Gas Bill [B6-2026]

Repeals the Gas Act 2001 and writes a new statute for piped gas, LNG and gas-to-power.

What it is

The Bill modernises the gas regulatory framework: licensing across the chain (transmission, storage, distribution, liquefaction, regasification), stronger NERSA oversight, and a legal path for gas-to-power and imported LNG. DEE briefed the Portfolio Committee on Electricity and Energy on 6 May 2026; the committee adopted a motion of desirability on 13 May.

Committee issues already on the record

  • Affordability and tariff regulation in a concentrated market.
  • The “gas cliff” — DEE said it would return before year-end 2026 with timelines to secure supply and infrastructure.
  • Alignment with IRP 2025 gas-to-power volumes and with SAWEM (gas plants as dispatchable backup to wind and solar).

What to watch

Hearings and the DEE reply sitting are done. Next is clause-by-clause / committee report, then National Assembly, NCOP (it is a s76 bill), and assent. That diary is not published. Watch the PCEE programme, not NERSA.
