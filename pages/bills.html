SAWEM Trading Platform Framework, 2026

Open

NERSA · issued 28 Sep 2026 · public comments due 16:00 on 31 Oct 2026 · virtual hearing 19 Nov 2026

The digital-platform rulebook for SAWEM: NERSA must approve the Market Operator’s systems before commercial operation. Fully automated algorithmic trading without manual review is barred for the first five years after launch. Energy Council members send comments to the Council by 27 October; NERSA’s public window runs to 31 October (MSU@nersa.org.za).

Why it matters: This is the software-and-governance layer next to the Market Code. NTCSA cannot go live in April 2027 without an approved platform.

Revised Electricity Pricing Policy (EPP) 2026

Open

DEE · issued 28 Aug 2026 · comments due 28 Oct 2026 (extended by GN 7948 / GG 55436 from 28 Sep)

Cabinet-approved replacement of the 2008 Electricity Pricing Policy. It is the pricing rulebook for the move into SAWEM: cost-reflective, unbundled tariffs; NERSA must publish a 10-year price path; inefficiencies cannot be passed through to customers; free basic electricity for qualifying poor households rises from 50 kWh to 200–300 kWh a month.

Why it matters: MYPD 7 and wholesale tariffs will be written against this policy. It is the document that decides how generation, networks, legacy costs and subsidies show up on a bill.

Draft NERSA Rules on Electricity Trading

Input Consolidation

NERSA · issued 26 Jun 2026 · comments closed 28 Sep 2026 (extended from 29 Jul 2026)

Rules under sections 35(1) and 35(3) of the Electricity Regulation Act for who may trade electricity, how retail competition is phased in, and how traders relate to the Market Code. Version 3 (May 2026) covers Phases 1–2 of retail competition; Phases 3–4 are sketched in appendices. Once the Market Code is approved it prevails over these rules for SAWEM participants.

Why it matters: This is the licence-and-conduct layer for traders, wheeling, PPAs and (later) customer switching. NERSA is consolidating Version 3 inputs; no hearing date has been announced for the extended round.

Gas Bill [B6-2026]

Input Consolidation

Parliament / DEE · introduced 5 Mar 2026 · comments closed 7 Aug 2026 · PCEE hearings 11–12 Aug · DEE responses 26 Aug 2026

Repeals the Gas Act 2001 and rebuilds the piped-gas framework: stronger NERSA powers, investment-ready licensing, and a legal home for gas-to-power, LNG and new infrastructure. Transitional Generation Pricing & Vesting Contract Framework

Input Consolidation

NERSA · issued 20 May 2026 · comments closed 4 Aug 2026 · public hearing 19 Aug 2026

The shock absorber for SAWEM. Vesting contracts are NERSA-priced hedges between NTCSA (via the Central Purchasing Agency) and generators/distributors so a sudden free market does not blow up Eskom’s revenue or customer prices. Transition is described as 5–10 years, stepping down toward 2030. NERSA decides contract prices, duration and unwind. No Energy Regulator decision date is published.

Wholesale Electricity Pricing Methodology

Input Consolidation

NERSA · issued 18 May 2026 · comments closed 4 Aug 2026 · public hearing 14 Aug 2026

How the wholesale price is built: time-of-use energy, a generation capacity charge, and a legacy charge. It is the methodology that turns MYPD-style generation revenue into the prices sitting behind vesting contracts and, later, SAWEM. Pair it with the vesting framework — one is the price recipe, the other is the hedge.

Consultation on Section 29 of the Electricity Regulation Act

Input Consolidation

NERSA · issued 29 Jun 2026 · comments closed 3 Aug 2026

Section 29 is the Minister’s power (with NERSA) to determine new generation capacity — the legal hook for IRP procurement, Ministerial determinations, and who may build what. The consultation is about how that power is used once a competitive market exists, so government procurement does not collide with SAWEM.

Eskom MYPD5 — Regulatory Clearing Account FY2024/25

Input Consolidation

NERSA · issued 29 Jun 2026 · comments closed 14 Aug 2026

The RCA is the true-up on a closed MYPD year: what Eskom actually spent and sold versus what NERSA allowed. An approved RCA amount is recovered (or returned) through later tariffs. This application is the last MYPD5 year true-up and will feed the tariff path that the new EPP says NERSA must forecast.

NTCSA Market Code application

Input Consolidation

NERSA · issued 21 Apr 2026 · comments closed 17 Jun 2026 · ELS hearing 1 Jul 2026 · SAWEM go-live now April 2027

NTCSA, as licensed Market Operator, applied for NERSA approval of the SAWEM Market Code (rev 2.3, Feb 2026). The Code is the rulebook for the day-ahead market, balancing, settlement, participation and the Central Purchasing Agency. On 30 September 2026 NTCSA said go-live is now April 2027 (delayed from April 2026) and that it is standing up a ring-fenced market-surveillance unit as a Market Operator licence condition. Approval of the Code is still the legal on-switch.

Cadastral system (land + mining)

Reform in progress

DLRRD · DMPR · eDRS live 1 Apr 2025 · B27-2026 in the National Assembly · mining cadastre national target 31 Mar 2027

South Africa runs two cadastres. The land cadastre (Chief Surveyor-General and Deeds) is the register of parcels, servitudes and title. The mining cadastre (DMPR, replacing SAMRAD) is the register of prospecting and mining rights. eDRS is in a five-year dual paper/electronic phase; Bill B27-2026 writes the once-electronic-always-electronic rule. The Western Cape mining cadastre is live; national rollout is a Government–Business Partnership deliverable for 31 March 2027.

Why it matters: Grid and gas servitudes, SEZ land, mining-versus-surface rights, and land-reform title all sit on this stack.
