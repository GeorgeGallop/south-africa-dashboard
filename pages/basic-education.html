🎓 Department of Basic Education

Schools & education policy

📑 Overview

The Department of Basic Education (DBE) is the national department responsible for the administration of primary and secondary schooling in South Africa. It sets national education policy, curriculum standards and manages the schooling system.

📄 Functions
  • Developing and implementing national education policy
  • Setting the national curriculum and assessment standards
  • Managing the National Senior Certificate (matric) examinations
  • Administering school funding and infrastructure programmes
  • Promoting early childhood development and literacy
  • Coordinating the school nutrition programme
