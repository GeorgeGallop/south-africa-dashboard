IRP2025

Integrated Resource Plan 2025: South Africa's updated energy roadmap with a stronger focus on gas, renewables, and a managed coal phase-out.

Core focus areas
  • Gas generation: stronger policy support for gas-fired capacity to provide flexible, firm power as renewables grow.
  • Renewables expansion: accelerated procurement for solar and wind to boost clean energy supply.
  • Coal phase-out: clearer signalling for reducing reliance on older coal plants while protecting system stability.
  • Storage and flexibility: increased emphasis on batteries, hybrid plants, and peaking capacity.
  • Regional cooperation: continued use of imports and cross-border trade to diversify supply sources.

General analysis: The IRP balance plan strongly prioritises solar and wind additions, with more than 70 GW of renewables proposed over the planning horizon. Gas IPP growth is also significant, adding over 15 GW of flexible capacity, while nuclear is targeted at roughly 5 GW and pumped storage at 1.3 GW. In total, the plan points to a major shift away from new coal build, instead leaning on renewables, storage, and gas to meet future demand.

Coal retirement path

The IRP includes a coal retirement modelling comparison. Under a 50-year retirement profile, coal capacity falls more rapidly, creating a gap of roughly 15.2 GW versus a 60-year lifetime path by 2046, and still about 8.7 GW by 2050.

This coal-life gap is a major system driver: a shorter retirement profile requires earlier replacement capacity and more aggressive renewables and gas deployment, while a longer life delays the need for this replacement.

Why this update matters

The 2025 IRP update reflects South Africa’s shift toward a more balanced and resilient power system. It combines renewable growth with gas-based flexibility and a phased reduction in coal dependence, helping to support economic development while improving reliability.
