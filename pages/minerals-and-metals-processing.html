What this covers

Minerals and metals processing is the largest manufacturing domain in South Africa's industrial base. It starts where mining ends — taking run-of-mine ore and upgrading it through concentration, smelting, refining and alloying into the metals and materials that supply steelmaking, automotive, construction, electronics and export markets.

This chain is responsible for the bulk of South Africa's mineral value-add before export and is the foundation of the country's metals-based manufacturing competitiveness. Keeping more of the chain onshore — rather than exporting raw ore — is central to industrial policy.

The full process chain, step by step
  1. Mining — ore extracted from the ground. Mining section →
  2. Concentration — ore crushed, milled and upgraded to concentrate. Concentration →
  3. Smelting — concentrate heated to separate metal from slag. Smelting →
  4. Refining and purification — producing commercially pure metals and chemical intermediates. Refining →
  5. Alloying, steel and ferroalloy production — blending metals for steel, stainless steel and specialty alloys. Alloying →

Downstream customers include the steel industry, automotive manufacturing, construction, electrical equipment, and export commodity markets.

Why the chain matters for South Africa
  • Value capture. Each stage adds value that would otherwise be earned by an overseas processor. Beneficiating before export keeps margin, jobs and tax onshore.
  • Energy intensity. Smelting and refining are among the most electricity-intensive industrial activities in the economy — which is why they are tightly bound to Eskom's tariffs and the power supply debate.
  • Industrial base. Steel (ArcelorMittal), stainless (Columbus), ferroalloys (Samancor, Glencore–Merafe, Assmang) and precious-metals refining (Rand Refinery, Amplats) are the anchor industries of the domestic metals economy.
  • Structural pressure. Cheap imported steel, electricity prices and competition from Asian ferroalloy producers have forced plant closures and restarts across the chain in recent years.
