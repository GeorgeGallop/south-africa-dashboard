👩 Commission for Gender Equality

Gender equality promotion

📑 Overview

The Commission for Gender Equality (CGE) is a Chapter 9 institution established under the Commission on Gender Equality Act of 1996. It promotes, monitors and evaluates the status of gender equality in South Africa, and works to transform society towards gender parity.

📄 Functions
  • Promoting gender equality and the empowerment of women
  • Monitoring government policies and legislation for gender sensitivity
  • Investigating complaints of gender discrimination
  • Conducting research and public education on gender issues
  • Advising Parliament and the executive on gender-related matters
  • Litigating on behalf of victims of gender discrimination
